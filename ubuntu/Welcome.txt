Welcome to your Ubuntu tablet

The buttons at the top left open Tablet controls, Files, Text editor and Terminal.
Tablet controls shows battery, Wi-Fi, storage and a brightness slider.

Your documents belong in your Documents folder.
Everyday apps run as the account chosen during setup. Administration remains available over SSH.
For local sudo access, choose "Set local password / enable sudo" in Tablet
controls, then open a NEW Terminal window. SSH password login remains disabled.

The on-screen keyboard appears when a supported app's text field has focus.
Use Space or Enter to finish a word. ABC toggles capitals; ?123 shows symbols.

This is still a prototype: graphics are software-rendered. Audio and suspend
have not been validated. Keep it charging during setup.
